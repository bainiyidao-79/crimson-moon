Quick answers to the questions players are actually searching about Crimson Moon, with honest "to be confirmed" markers where launch-week information is still thin. This page is updated as verified facts land.

## What Is Crimson Moon?

A gothic action-adventure RPG from independent studio ProbablyMonsters. You play a Nephilim — a human-angel hybrid — clearing zones of the fallen city of Gildenarch for the Order of the Crimson Moon, with soulslike-leaning melee and a roguelike run structure. Full context in [what is Crimson Moon](/intro/what-is-crimson-moon).

## When Did It Come Out, and on What Platforms?

**September 1, 2026**, simultaneously on PC (Steam), PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S. Details in [release date and platforms](/release/release-date-and-platforms).

## Is It a Soulslike?

Partly — it's a hybrid. Deliberate melee and brutal bosses lean soulslike; the run structure, boons, and hub upgrades lean roguelike. The Outerhaven's review line is still the cleanest summary: "a soulslike and a roguelike had a baby." More in our [genre guide](/guide/soulslike-or-roguelike).

## Does It Have Co-Op and Crossplay?

Yes and yes. It is **2-player online co-op**, with **crossplay across PC, PS5, and Xbox Series X|S** and a crossplay on/off setting. Matchmaking fine print when the toggle is off is still being mapped by players — see the [crossplay guide](/guide/crossplay-and-co-op).

## Is There a Demo?

There was a pre-launch demo (one loadout, the hub blacksmith, a slice of Gildenarch). Whether it remains downloadable after launch is **to be confirmed** — see the [demo guide](/guide/demo-guide).

## How Much Does It Cost?

Base game **$19.99**. Deluxe Edition Upgrade **$9.99** (Mahkten Armor Set, the guitar-shaped Axe of the Gods, and entitlement to a post-launch expansion). Soundtrack DLC **$9.99**. Breakdown in [price and editions](/release/price-and-editions).

## How Long Is Crimson Moon?

**To be confirmed.** No official length figure exists yet, and no solid playtime data has aggregated in launch week. One previewer's honest framing: "I wasn't expecting some enormous AAA production with 100 hours of handcrafted content. What is here is already worth the asking price." We will add real numbers when they exist.

## Can My PC Run It?

Minimum: Windows 11, Core i5-8400 / Ryzen 5 2600, 16 GB RAM, GTX 1070 / RX 5700 / Arc A580, 50 GB storage. Recommended: RTX 3070-class GPU and 32 GB RAM. Full tables in [system requirements](/guide/pc-system-requirements).

## Is It Any Good?

Split, honestly. Steam user reviews sat at **Mixed — 59% of 1,806 English reviews positive** as of September 7, 2026, while press was warmer: GameTyrant ("killer game, killer price"), The Outerhaven, Gaming ProMax ("worth your $20"), and CGMagazine all reviewed positively at launch. The studio shipped patch 1.0.0.1 on September 3 after announcing fixes five hours into release, so expect movement.
