Yes — Crimson Moon had a **playable demo** before launch. Preview builds let players take a Nephilim through a slice of the full loop: a hub, the portal into Gildenarch, and zone-by-zone combat. The full game launched on September 1, 2026, so the demo era is now behind the game — whether the demo remains downloadable on the Steam page is **to be confirmed**.

## What the Demo Contained

Based on hands-on accounts from the preview window:

- **One basic loadout** — there was only one in the demo, so build experimentation was off the table
- **The hub** with the **blacksmith upgrade system** already in place — each zone clear fed upgrades
- **A portal drop into Gildenarch** to fight demons, undead, and Hellgrowth enemies zone by zone
- The core loop that survived into the full game: clear zones, collect rewards, upgrade, go back in

## What the Demo Didn't Show

Hands-on accounts noted the demo did not fully explain its loot or progression approach — the **Purify Loot** system and deeper build crafting only became clear with the full release. If you tried the demo and bounced off it, the [full gameplay guide](/guide/gameplay-and-combat) covers the systems the demo kept hidden.

## Demo vs. Full Game

The full game adds everything the demo held back: multiple loadouts and builds, the complete Ward structure of Gildenarch, the Savage Dead Gods as boss targets, 2-player online co-op, and the Nephilim mode loop with boon stacking. The base game is **$19.99** on Steam — see [price and editions](/release/price-and-editions) for the Deluxe options.
