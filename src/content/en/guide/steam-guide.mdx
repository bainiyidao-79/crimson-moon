Crimson Moon's Steam home is **app 4317690**, priced at **$19.99** for the base game. It is the PC version of the September 1, 2026 release, and the Steam build carries the full feature set: single-player, online co-op, achievements, cloud saves, and controller support.

## Steam Features at a Glance

- **30 Steam achievements** to chase across your runs
- **Steam Cloud** saves and **Family Sharing** both supported
- **Full controller support** with gamepad recommended, plus DualShock and DualSense support
- **8 supported languages**: English (audio), French, Italian, German, Spanish (Latin America), Japanese, Portuguese-Brazil, and Simplified Chinese

## Editions Available on Steam

The store page offers three ways to buy: the **base game ($19.99)**, the **Crimson Moon: Deluxe Edition Upgrade ($9.99)** that adds the Mahkten Armor Set, the Axe of the Gods, and a post-launch content expansion entitlement, and the **Crimson Moon Soundtrack ($9.99)** as separate DLC. There is also a **Crimson Moon Deluxe Edition bundle** that packages the base game with the upgrade — Steam's "Compare editions" table on the store page lays out the differences line by line. Our [price and editions guide](/release/price-and-editions) walks through what each tier actually includes.

## The Review Picture on Steam

Honest numbers, as of September 7, 2026: Steam shows **English Reviews: Mixed — 59% of 1,806 user reviews positive**. That is a launch-week snapshot of a days-old game, and the studio moved early on feedback (patch 1.0.0.1 landed September 3, with fixes announced on Discord five hours after release). Press reviews were warmer — The Outerhaven called it "a soulslike and a roguelike had a baby," and GameTyrant went with "killer game, killer price."
