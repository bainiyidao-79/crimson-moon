Crimson Moon is developed and published by **ProbablyMonsters**, an independent game company. The studio's official channels confirm it is self-publishing the gothic action RPG, which launched on September 1, 2026 for PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S.

## Who Is ProbablyMonsters?

ProbablyMonsters describes itself as an independent game company with "a unique way of inspiring creativity." The company runs its own publishing operation — Crimson Moon's official site, account system, and customer support (via crimsonmoon.zendesk.com) all live under the probablymonsters.com umbrella, so the studio is handling the game directly rather than relying on an outside publisher.

## Team Metal: The Crimson Moon Team

Within the studio, the group building Crimson Moon goes by **Team Metal**. The name shows up in the game's official Discord communication — for example, a launch-week message addressed players in Gildenarch and noted that "Team Metal is actively monitoring your feedback and reports." That responsiveness was visible early: the studio announced fixes **five hours into release** via Discord, and Steam patch 1.0.0.1 followed on September 3, 2026.

## What Else Is ProbablyMonsters Working On?

Crimson Moon is not the studio's only project. The official site also lists **Nekome: Nazi Hunter**, a separate title with its own announce trailer. The two games run in parallel under the ProbablyMonsters umbrella.
