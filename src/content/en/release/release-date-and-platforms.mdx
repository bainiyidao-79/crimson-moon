**Crimson Moon released on September 1, 2026**, and it was a simultaneous launch: the game went live the same day on **PC (Steam), PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S**. The official launch trailer said it plainly — "Crimson Moon is OUT NOW on PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S."

## The Release Date, Verified Three Ways

September 1, 2026 is confirmed by the Steam release, the **official launch trailer** uploaded to YouTube on September 1 with an "OUT NOW" description, and launch-day reviews from GameTyrant, The Outerhaven, Gaming ProMax, and CGMagazine — all dated September 1. No region-staggered unlock windows were announced; PC and consoles went live together.

## PC (Steam)

Steam is not the only way to play on PC, either: the studio's official buy links list Crimson Moon, the Deluxe Edition, and the soundtrack on the **Epic Games Store** alongside Steam. The achievements and store specifics covered across this site refer to the Steam build.
The PC version lives at Steam app 4317690 for **$19.99**. It carries 30 achievements, Steam Cloud, Family Sharing, and full controller support with DualShock/DualSense support listed. PC players should check the [official system requirements](/guide/pc-system-requirements) — Windows 11, a GTX 1070-class GPU, and 50 GB of storage are the minimum floor.

## PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S

Both consoles got the game day-and-date with PC, with online co-op included on every platform. Full controller support extends across the board (DualSense on PS5, and the Steam build explicitly lists DualShock and DualSense support for players using those pads on PC). Crossplay links the three platforms together in 2-player co-op — details in the [crossplay and co-op guide](/guide/crossplay-and-co-op).

## Post-Launch Support Has Already Started

The studio didn't treat launch day as the finish line: fixes were announced on Discord **five hours into release**, and Steam patch 1.0.0.1 shipped on September 3, 2026. A post-launch content expansion is also part of the Deluxe Edition plan — see [price and editions](/release/price-and-editions).
